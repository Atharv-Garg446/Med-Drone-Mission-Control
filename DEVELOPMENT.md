### What I decided / designed

- **Discrete-Event Simulation over Static Routing**: Real-world drone missions fail on edge cases that static CVRP formulations ignore: battery depletion against headwinds, sudden airspace closures, and urgent unscheduled delivery requests. I designed a discrete-event simulator (`simulate_fleet.py`) tracking live per-drone state vectors (GPS position, remaining energy/range budget, cargo mass, active payload temperature timers).
- **Fast Full Re-Solve over Complex Graph Splices**: Instead of building delicate graph-repair heuristics when an airborne disruption occurs, I focused on optimizing the end-to-end solver pipeline (obstacle-avoiding A* flight matrix + CVRP heuristics + 2-opt) to execute in approximately 0.20–0.79 s across the tested 8–15-stop scenarios. Airborne drones become moving origin nodes with their live remaining payload and range budgets, producing constraint-checked fleet reassignments from the current mission state.
- **Hierarchical Airspace Geometry & Safety Margins**: Direct flight is assumed for clear air, but intersecting polygon no-fly zones (NFZs/TFRs) drops into local 8-connected grid A* (`nofly_astar.py`). To enforce a 50 m safety clearance without multi-second raster scans, I designed conservative axis-aligned bounding box (AABB) pre-filtering for polygon distance checks, spatial grid caching across symmetric route pairs, and cell half-diagonal compensation when `MAX_CELLS = 120` coarsens the grid. Bidirectional string-pulling smooths discrete raster staircasing into clean flight corridors.
- **Constrained Multi-Heuristic CVRP Engine**: Implemented Nearest Neighbor, Urgency-Weighted Nearest Neighbor, and Clarke-Wright Savings with 2-opt post-optimization (`vrp_scratch.py`). Instead of unconstrained solving followed by post-hoc filtering, physical limits (payload capacity, return-to-base range budget, cold-chain temperature thresholds, delivery time windows) are verified at every insertion step with immediate infeasibility diagnosis.
- **Decoupled Landing Zone Vision Screening**: Isolated landing site obstruction detection (`yolo_safety.py`) from the core flight routing engine. An aerial snapshot is processed as a pre-landing gate—classifying obstacles like vehicles and people to trigger immediate holding patterns or route aborts without blocking pathfinding computation.
- **Standard Autopilot Export & Real SITL Flight-Stack Integration**: Built direct export to QGroundControl WPL 110 format (`waypoint_export.py`) and integrated with real ArduCopter SITL firmware over MAVLink (`flightstack/`). Telemetry state (`telemetry.py`) strictly segregates simulator ground truth from autopilot EKF state estimates, rejecting stale or un-aided position data. Supported both hover-and-release and land-and-deliver modes.
- **Airspace Source Grounding & Credential Gating**: Designed `airspace_provider.py` with temporal validity windows, GeoJSON ingestion, and cached fallback with `stale=True` flags. Gated the Digital Sky flight-plan intersection check by credentials and official schemas, refusing to scrape undocumented private endpoints or fabricate fake geometry.
- **First-Principles Vehicle Mass & Energy Modeling**: Created `flightstack/vehicle_model.py` incorporating MTOW structural limits and momentum-theory induced power ($P \propto m^{1.5}$) plus parasite drag ($P \propto v^3$), evaluating leg-by-leg battery consumption as cargo is dropped off.
- **Dynamic Obstacle Tracking & Conflict Prediction**: Created `flightstack/obstacle_tracker.py` computing 3D relative kinematics and Closest Point of Approach ($t_{\text{cpa}}$, $d_{\text{cpa}}$), issuing tactical LOITER holds and converting persistent hazards into inflated exclusion zones for A* bypass.
- **Precision Landing Visual Transforms**: Built `flightstack/precision_landing.py` transforming downward-facing camera optical coordinates to vehicle Body-NED frames, formatting MAVLink `LANDING_TARGET` messages with graceful fallbacks.
